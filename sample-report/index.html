Brand A in ChatGPT: free AI visibility check

This is a real free check we prepared in November 2026, with the brand's name removed. It's exactly what you receive when you request yours: a short email-length report, no call needed.

Summary

ChatGPT names Brand A first when a shopper asks for the best travel backpack with no budget. Add "under $300" and Brand A disappears, even though its lighter 40L backpack sells for $250.

In our test, Brand A showed up in 2 of 4 buyer questions where it has a fitting product. Both misses were budget questions, and in both the shopper was sent to REI, Amazon and the big outdoor brands instead.

The lighter 40L costs $250 on Brand A's site, so it fits both budget questions on price and size. Brand A's product pages already carry review ratings and GTINs in their structured data, which is a good base.

Why it matters

Most shoppers ask with a budget in mind, so the "under $300" questions are likely where most buying intent sits. Right now ChatGPT seems to file Brand A as the premium pick and hands the budget shopper to Osprey, Patagonia, Cotopaxi and Thule.

In both budget answers, ChatGPT sent the shopper to REI and Amazon rather than to a brand's own store. A Brand A mention there would point to Brand A's own site instead.

What we would fix first

  1. Say the budget out loud on the lighter 40L page. State the price band, carry-on fit and one-bag use in the title area, description and an FAQ: "a 40L carry-on travel backpack under $300". Assistants match products to the words in the question.
  2. Publish an honest comparison of the two 40L packs. One page that answers "which one should I buy on a budget?" gives ChatGPT a clear source that links the lighter pack to the budget question.
  3. Check the product data AI channels read. Make sure the lighter pack's price, availability and its own reviews sit on its page and in the Shopify product feed, separate from the flagship.
  4. Get the lighter pack into "best under $300" roundups. The brands ChatGPT named for budget questions tend to appear in many third-party lists. Pitching to those writers is the slower but stronger lever.
  5. Re-test monthly. Run the same questions again to see whether the lighter pack starts showing up.

How we tested

We asked ChatGPT each question as a US shopper would, in November 2026, and recorded the brands named and the stores linked. AI answers change between runs, so treat this as a snapshot, not a ranking. A full audit repeats each question several times and covers 30+ questions.
