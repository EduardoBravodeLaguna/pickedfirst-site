FAQ

Questions brands ask us

Is this just SEO with a new name?

It overlaps, but it isn't the same. Google ranks pages; AI assistants pick a handful of products and explain why. That means product details like price band, size, materials and use case need to be stated clearly enough for an assistant to match them to a shopper's question.

Which AI assistants do you test?

ChatGPT first, because that's where most AI shopping questions happen today. We also check Perplexity and Google's AI answers for your key questions.

Do you need access to my store?

Not for the free check: we only use your public site. For the setup we ask for Shopify collaborator access, limited to products and content, and you can remove it anytime.

Who is this for?

Independent Shopify brands selling in the US, especially in outdoor, travel and gear categories, where shoppers ask detailed "best X under $Y" questions.

How fast will I see changes?

Product page and data fixes are live within days. How fast assistants pick them up varies, which is why we re-test every month and report what changed.
