Access and scope

What I can touch in your store, and what I can't

To improve a product page, I need some access to your Shopify store, or none at all if you'd rather paste the changes yourself. This page says exactly what that means, so you can decide before saying yes.

Applies to the free pilot and to paid work. Eduardo Bravo de Laguna, founder of PickedFirst.

Two ways to work together

Option A: collaborator access

  • I send a collaborator request from my Shopify Partner account
  • You choose the permissions before accepting it
  • I make the approved changes myself
  • You remove the access whenever you want

Option B: ready to paste

  • No access to your store at all
  • I send every change written out, with the exact place it goes
  • Your team pastes it in
  • I check the live page afterwards

The only permissions I ask for

PermissionWhy I need it
ProductsTo edit the product title, description, specs and FAQ of the products we agreed on.
Online Store: themesTo check structured data and add an FAQ block, always on a duplicate of your theme first.
Online Store: pagesOnly if we agree on an answer page, such as a comparison guide.

What I never ask for or touch

No access to

  • Orders and customer data
  • Payments, payouts and billing
  • Staff accounts and passwords
  • Apps and integrations

No changes to

  • Prices, discounts or promotions
  • Brand wording or claims you tell me to keep
  • Any product we didn't agree on
  • Your live theme, until you approve the preview

How a change goes live

1

Proposal

You get each change in writing: the text today, the new text and why.

2

Your OK

Nothing is published until you approve it by email. You can change or reject anything.

3

Preview

Theme changes are made on a duplicate theme and shared as a preview link first.

4

Logged

Every published change is logged with its date and the old text, so any of it can be undone.

Removing access

You can remove me at any time in your Shopify admin, under Settings, Users, Collaborators. At the end of the pilot I'll ask you to remove the access unless we keep working together.

Your data

I don't download customer or order data. What I keep is screenshots of ChatGPT's answers and your public product pages. Your report is shared only with you, and I only mention your brand publicly, named or anonymized, if you say yes.

My honest promise: nobody can guarantee a spot in ChatGPT's answers. What I can promise is that I only change what we agreed on, only after you approve it, and that you'll see exactly what changed.

Questions about access?

Ask before you decide. I'll reply within a day, and there's no call needed.
